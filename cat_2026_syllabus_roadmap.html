The Adaptive Post-Mock Decision Engine: Real-Time Sectional Rebalancing

Post-Mock Action Protocol

Day 1 established your baseline; every subsequent mock must dynamically modify your next 7 days of study. Follow this strict triage logic after every single mock:

Bucket A: Strength (Green) >80% Accuracy

Topics where solve speed is fast (<2.5m in QA, <10m in DILR) and accuracy is high.

Action: Cut daily study time to maintenance mode (1 drill every 3 days). Reallocate freed time to Bucket B.
Bucket B: Swing Zone (Yellow) 50–70% Accuracy

Topics where you invest high time (>3m/Q or >12m/set) but fall into traps or make algebraic calculation slips.

Action: Allocate +45 to +60 minutes/day for the next 7 days. This is where percentile leaps happen.
Bucket C: Black Holes (Red) <40% / Negative

Topics where attempts consistently yield negative marks or burn >4 minutes without reaching an answer.

Action: HARD DROP from active mock attempts. Do not let stubborn pride destroy exam-day sectional scores.
