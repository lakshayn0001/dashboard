Official Specification Analysis

Does IIM or IIT Publish an Official CAT Syllabus?

Official Fact Check
✕ The Reality: No Prescribed Chapter List

Neither the conducting IIMs (Ahmedabad, Calcutta, Bangalore, Lucknow, etc.) nor participating IITs (Bombay, Delhi, Madras, Kharagpur, Roorkee, Kanpur) publish an official textbook or chapter-wise syllabus for CAT. Any website claiming an "Official Chapter Syllabus" is publishing an unofficial compilation.

What IIM officially publishes: The CAT Information Bulletin & Test Structure specifying section names, question count, and 40-minute sectional windows.
✓ The Empirical Syllabus (2017–2025 Past Papers)

Because CAT strictly tests aptitude rather than memorization, the real syllabus is derived through empirical analysis of actual CAT papers from 2017 through 2025. Every question in this roadmap is categorized directly from past examination slots.

● Class 8–10 NCERT Math foundation + Reading Comprehension + Unconventional DILR sets.

🏛️ IIT Management Schools & CAT Cutoff Truth for Engineers

IIT management departments (e.g. DoMS IIT Madras, DMS IIT Delhi, SJMSOM IIT Bombay, VGSoM IIT Kharagpur, DoMS IIT Roorkee, IME IIT Kanpur) admit candidates exclusively through CAT scores. Because IITs place high value on technical undergraduate degrees (B.Tech CSE), your engineering foundation gives you an advantage in their shortlisting criteria compared to older IIMs that penalize Class 12 percentages.

DoMS IIT Madras ↗ DMS IIT Delhi ↗ SJMSOM IIT Bombay ↗ VGSoM IIT Kharagpur ↗ DoMS IIT Roorkee ↗ IME IIT Kanpur ↗

The Execution Blueprint

The Universal 4-Step Study Loop (For Every Single Topic)

Do NOT watch 10 videos and call it study. Follow this strict sequence:

1 30–40 Min

Learn the Core Concept

Watch ONE targeted video of THAT topic only (e.g. 2IIM or Rodha English). Never binge multiple topics in one session.

2 30 Min

Solve Foundational Easy

Solve 15–25 untimed Level of Difficulty 1 (LOD 1) questions without a stopwatch to build clean muscle memory.

3 30 Min

Solve Exam-Like PYQs

Tackle 10–15 mixed CAT previous-year questions from that specific topic (CAT 2017–2025 papers on 2IIM/Cracku).

4 10 Min

Log Mistakes in Error Log

Document precisely why you missed the question (calculation slip, concept gap, or trap option). That is the real study.

📖 Part A: Reading Comprehension (~16 Questions, 4 Passages)

Each passage is 400–700 words with 4 questions. Topics CAT loves: philosophy, psychology, economics, history, sociology, biology, art & culture. Question types: Main idea, Author's tone, Inference, "Which option is supported", "Which is NOT true". There is no grammar chapter list here; the syllabus is the muscle of analytical reading.

✍️ Part B: Verbal Ability (~8 Questions)

🚫 Skip as a syllabus: Direct grammar worksheets, idioms, and 1000-word vocabulary lists. CAT does not ask direct vocab MCQs anymore.
SECTION 2

Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning (22 Questions)

Paper presents 4 or 5 SETS. Your entire goal is to identify and complete 2 CLEAN sets (90+ percentile benchmark).

40 Min Locked

High-Impact Time Savers: What to Actively Ignore
1. Group Discussions & Interviews

Spoken English, GDs, essays, and SOPs are for interview calls AFTER November. Zero points inside the CAT exam hall.

2. Grammar Worksheets & Vocab Lists

Direct grammar rules and memorizing 1000 flashcards are almost never tested directly in modern CAT papers.

3. Advanced Calculus & Trig Identities

Class 11–12 integration, differential calculus, and trigonometry identities do NOT belong in CAT Quantitative Aptitude.

4. Hoarding 5 Different Quant Books

One comprehensive book (Arun Sharma LOD 1 & 2) + CAT 2017–2025 actual papers is more than enough.

5. DILR Overwhelm in Week 1

Do not attempt to master complex tournament tables on day one. Clean numerical tables + 1 arrangement family is ideal.

6. Olympiad Tricks & Rare Theorems

Skip hyper-complex number theory tricks, tough 3D geometry coordinate theorems, and LOD 3 edge cases.
