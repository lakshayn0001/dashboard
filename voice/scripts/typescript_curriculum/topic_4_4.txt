Audio briefing for 4.4 Union-to-intersection & contravariance tricks. Core concept: Function parameter positions infer as intersections under conditional types; this power tool enables merging unions into unified record types. Key principles: These are library-author tools. Readable app code should rarely contain them inline. Why this matters in production: Required for senior production TypeScript and library architecture. Common pitfall to avoid: Dropping unreadable one-liners into product code. Practical takeaway: Placing union constituents in function parameter positions forces the compiler to infer their intersection to satisfy contravariance.
