Audio briefing for 1.12 Type assertions (as) — first rules. Core concept: Type assertions ('as T') do not perform any runtime casting or conversions. They are compile-time overrides that can introduce silent runtime crashes. Key principles: Assertions are compile-time only. as is a smell at trust boundaries; prefer narrowing or Zod. Why this matters in production: You will see assertions everywhere; you must know when they are lies. Common pitfall to avoid: Asserting instead of fixing the model. as any to silence errors. Practical takeaway: Avoid using '!' because Map.get() can miss at runtime, leading to null pointer exceptions.
