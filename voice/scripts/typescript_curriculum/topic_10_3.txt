Audio briefing for 10.3 Architectural System Defense: Migrating JS Monoliths to Strict TypeScript. Core concept: Migrating legacy codebases requires a phased, automated pipeline: JSDoc opt-in → allowJs/checkJs → leaf-to-root TS migration → strict CI thresholds. Key principles: Never attempt a 'big bang' migration; product features must ship continuously during migration. Migrate leaf utilities and shared types first, followed by middleware and core business logic, and finally UI components. Why this matters in production: Senior engineering candidates are evaluated on real-world refactoring strategy, risk mitigation, and team productivity trade-offs. Common pitfall to avoid: Proposing to rewrite everything in TypeScript from scratch. Running 'tsc --init' on day one and getting 4,000 errors that block developer PRs. Practical takeaway: Leaf-to-root migration avoids circular dependencies and ensures types flow naturally upwards without relying on temporary 'any' casts.
