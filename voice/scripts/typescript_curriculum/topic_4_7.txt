Audio briefing for 4.7 Legacy features you must still recognize. Core concept: You will encounter namespaces and triple-slash directives in legacy enterprise repos and older DefinitelyTyped files; do not introduce them into modern code. Key principles: You will read these in old handbooks and old enterprise repos. Do not introduce them into a new React/Node app. Why this matters in production: Maintaining legacy codebases and older library typings. Common pitfall to avoid: Adding namespaces 'for organization' in modern app code. Practical takeaway: Namespaces were an early internal module system replaced by standard ECMAScript modules.
