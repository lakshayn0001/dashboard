Audio briefing for 3.16 JSX / TSX language support. Core concept: Always type component children as React.ReactNode (which accepts strings, portals, null, arrays) rather than restricting to JSX.Element. Key principles: TSX is TypeScript's JSX parse mode. react-jsx is the modern automatic runtime. Why this matters in production: All React/Next work. Common pitfall to avoid: Confusing JSX.Element with ReactNode (children often need ReactNode). Practical takeaway: JSX.Element represents only a single instantiated tag, rejecting plain strings, numbers, or fragment arrays.
