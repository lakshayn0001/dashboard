Audio briefing for 2.4 Discriminated unions. Core concept: Discriminated unions combine a literal tag (status) with an exhaustive switch to mathematically eliminate impossible states. Key principles: A shared literal tag (type / kind / status) lets TS narrow the whole object. Illegal combinations (success without data, error with data) can be made unrepresentable. Why this matters in production: The single highest-leverage modeling pattern in full-stack TS. Common pitfall to avoid: Using multiple independent booleans for mutually exclusive states. Forgetting a unique discriminant. Practical takeaway: The shared literal tag 'status' enables compiler narrowing, and the 'never' default guarantees compile-time exhaustiveness.
