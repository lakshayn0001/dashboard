Audio briefing for 5.1 Component props and children. Core concept: Prefer ComponentPropsWithoutRef<'button'> over React.FC. It cleanly forwards HTML attributes while preserving precise generic prop types. Key principles: Prefer type ButtonProps = React.ComponentPropsWithoutRef<'button'> & { variant: . }. React.FC implicitly adds children and has historically weak generic support. Why this matters in production: Design systems, wrapping Next Link / Image. Common pitfall to avoid: Using React.FC everywhere by habit. Forgetting to forward native props. Practical takeaway: Explicit prop definitions avoid React.FC's historical quirks and clearly declare required vs optional children.
