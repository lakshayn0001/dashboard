Audio briefing for 3.15 Decorators and metadata. Core concept: TypeScript 5.0+ supports standard TC39 stage-3 decorators; legacy NestJS/TypeORM services still rely on experimentalDecorators + emitDecoratorMetadata. Key principles: In many NestJS apps you still see the legacy decorator emit. Decorators are not required for React/Express apps. Why this matters in production: Only if you work with Nest/Angular-like DI. Otherwise Optional. Common pitfall to avoid: Enabling experimentalDecorators in an app that does not need them. Practical takeaway: TC39 Stage-3 decorators take target and context parameters without requiring legacy experimental flags.
