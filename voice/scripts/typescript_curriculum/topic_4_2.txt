Audio briefing for 4.2 Branded and opaque types. Core concept: Branded types use unique symbols to emulate nominal typing in a structural system, completely preventing accidental argument swaps. Key principles: type UserId = string & { readonly __brand: 'UserId' } makes accidental mixing impossible at compile time. Still erased at runtime; Zod/branded parsers should create them. Why this matters in production: IDs across a full-stack app, money amounts, email strings that were validated. Common pitfall to avoid: Branding without a parser (false safety). Over-branding every string until DX collapses. Practical takeaway: Nominal branding prevents accidental misuse of primitive strings while keeping runtime overhead at zero.
