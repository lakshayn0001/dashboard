Audio briefing for 3.9 Distributive conditional types (mastery). Core concept: When a type parameter is naked in a conditional (T extends .), it distributes across union members; wrapping it in a tuple [T] disables distribution. Key principles: Distribution is the engine behind Extract/Exclude and many key filters. You can map over unions by writing T extends any ? F<T> : never. Why this matters in production: Correctness of almost every advanced utility you will write. Common pitfall to avoid: Accidental distribution. Copy-pasting union-to-intersection without understanding. Practical takeaway: Wrapping a type parameter in a tuple [T] disables automatic distribution over union constituents.
