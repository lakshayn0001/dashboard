Audio briefing for 3.1 keyof type operator. Core concept: keyof produces a union of property keys; applied to a union of object shapes, it yields the intersection of keys common to all constituents. Key principles: keyof T is a union of property names. On a union, keyof is the intersection of keys (keys common to all). Why this matters in production: Form field names, sort keys, translators, query builders. Common pitfall to avoid: Assuming keyof of a union is all keys from every member. Practical takeaway: Combining keyof with generic constraints ensures that only valid property names can be passed.
