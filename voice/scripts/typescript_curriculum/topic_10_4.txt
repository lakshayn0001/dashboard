Audio briefing for 10.4 Compiler Performance Profiling & Diagnostic Troubleshooting. Core concept: Profile compiler bottlenecks using --generateTrace and prevent union explosion and runaway recursive instantiation. Key principles: Slow IDE autocompletion or CI builds are usually caused by combinatorial union explosion or deeply nested conditional types. Wrapping type parameters in 1-element tuples [T] prevents expensive distributive branching across union members. Why this matters in production: Essential knowledge for senior/staff engineers maintaining monorepos with hundreds of developers where compiler lag slows down CI. Common pitfall to avoid: Creating massive Cartesian unions of strings instead of structured objects or enums. Ignoring TS2589 errors and using // @ts-ignore instead of fixing recursion. Practical takeaway: Wrapping in [T] extends [any] treats the union as a single atomic unit, eliminating combinatorial branch explosion.
