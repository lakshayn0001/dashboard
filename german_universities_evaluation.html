Academic Profile Audit & ECTS Conversion

German admission committees perform a strict quantitative audit of your undergraduate transcript using the Bavarian formula.

Bavarian Grade Conversion Dual Routes

Formula: Grade = 1 + 3 × [(Nmax - Nd) / (Nmax - Nmin)]
Route 1: CGPA 10-Point Scale (CV Value)
• Nmax = 10.0 | Nmin = 4.0 (MDU Passing Grade 'P')
• Nd = 7.2 CGPA
Result = 1 + 3 × (2.8 / 6.0) = 2.40 ("Gut")
Route 2: Percentage Aggregate (Marks)
• Nmax = 100% | Nmin = 40% (MDU Passing Mark)
• Nd = 70.56% (3810 / 5400)
Result = 1 + 3 × (29.44 / 60) = 2.47 ("Gut")
Passau Official Evaluation Routes:
1. Internal Conversion: Passau converts foreign grades itself using its official conversion key (both 2.40 and 2.47 meet the 2.7 requirement).
2. Top 70% Cohort Alternative: Under Passau's admission statutes, admission requires a grade of 2.7 or better OR ranking in the best 70% of your graduating cohort (to be computed from official MDU cohort ranking).

Which postgraduate degree is most compatible?

MSc vs MBA: What are the key trade-offs?

In Germany, a public university M.Sc. offers €0 tuition, technical depth, and directly qualifies for the EU Blue Card (requires a qualifying job offer meeting statutory 2026 thresholds: €50,700 standard or €45,934.20 for STEM/IT shortage occupations).
