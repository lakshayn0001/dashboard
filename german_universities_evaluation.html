Academic Profile Audit & ECTS Conversion

German admission committees perform a strict quantitative audit of your undergraduate transcript using the Bavarian formula.

Bavarian Grade Conversion Standard

Formula: Grade = 1 + 3 × [(Nmax - Nd) / (Nmax - Nmin)]
• Nmax = 100% | Nmin = 40%
• Your Aggregate = 70.56% (3810 / 5400)
Converted Result = 1 + 3 × (29.44 / 60) = 2.47 ("Gut")

In Germany, 1.0 is top honors and 4.0 is the passing mark. Your 2.47 sits inside the "Gut" band and clears the 2.5 and 2.7 cutoffs enforced by top state universities.
